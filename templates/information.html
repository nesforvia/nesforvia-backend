About the 3rd Imperial Kingdom of Nesforvia

The 3rd Imperial Kingdom of Nesforvia (IKN) is an imperialist micronation located in West Yorkshire, England, formed through the union of the provinces of Garforvia and Nesfield. The current state represents the third major era of Nesforvian history, following multiple dissolutions, political restructures, and reunifications between its two founding leaders. The original Imperial Kingdom of Nesforvia was established in 2024 after Garforvia and Nesfield unified under a dual-emperor system. This became known retrospectively as the 1st Imperial Kingdom. The state operated for several months before dissolving in early 2025 due to internal inactivity and political decline. In early 2026, the project was revived as the 2nd Imperial Kingdom of Nesforvia, often shortened to IKN. The restored empire attempted to rebuild its governmental structure and public presence. However, instability returned after Emperor Max temporarily abdicated the throne and withdrew from micronational activity. Following this, Emperor Doom dissolved the state and founded a successor nation, the Imperium of Doomstadt. Doomstadt was led solely by Emperor Doom and adopted a far more centralised style of rule. Within parts of the micronational community, the state gained a reputation for being authoritarian, with critics describing it as a dictatorship. Despite this, the government remained active for a short period before negotiations on reunification began. On 10 May 2026, Emperor Max returned from his brief departure from micronationalism. Following discussions between Both emperors, Doomstadt was dissolved, and Garforvia and Nesfield were reunified once more, officially establishing The 3rd Imperial Kingdom of Nesforvia. The modern IKN continues to follow an imperialist identity centred around dual leadership, environmental themes, regional expansion, and the preservation of Nesforvian continuity across its different historical eras.

National overview

The flag of the 3rd Imperial Kingdom of Nesforvia represents the restoration and reunification of the empire following the collapse of previous Nesforvian states. Its dominant green colouring reflects the empire’s environmentalist and expansionist identity, while the white elements symbolise renewal, unity, and the return of stability after the dissolution of the 2nd Imperial Kingdom and the Imperium of Doomstadt. The central White Rose of Nesforvia acts as the primary national symbol, representing the unification of Garforvia and Nesfield and the continuation of the Nesforvian imperial legacy into its third era.

Nesforvian provinces

Garforvia

Garforvia is a Nesforvian province and was once a micronation. It unified with Nesfield to create Nesforvia. It is located in Altofts, West Yorkshire, near Wakefield and Leeds, and borders Normanton.

Ran by: @max_d_evans (Emperor Max I)

Nesfield

Nesfield is a Nesforvian province. It had not previously operated as a micronation, but unified with Garforvia to create Nesforvia. It is located in Belle Isle, Leeds.

Ran by: @.oliver2025 (Emperor Doom)
